## Warnings & Caveats

1. **Note sub-line data (2023 & 2024):** Detailed sub-line values within note sheets were not extracted. Main statement totals (BS, IS, CF) are fully populated and validated. Note sheets carry a comment directing to the source PDF for detail.
2. **2023 NCI:** Non-controlling interests had **net losses** in 2023 (NCI net profit = −286,608,490), reducing consolidated net profit.
3. **2023 equity structure:** "Saldo laba" is a single line (not split into appropriated/unappropriated). Mapped to the "unappropriated" row.
4. **Uang muka setoran modal (2023):** The advance for stock subscription (IDR 71.8B) is folded into the APIC row in the Balance Sheet. It was fully converted to APIC in 2024.
5. **Taksiran tagihan pajak (2023):** IDR 242,314,336 mapped to "Aset lain-lain" row (r25) — see BS mapping note above.
6. **2,000 IDR discrepancy:** The 2023 equity statement (p.178) shows total equity as IDR 742,645,972,247, while the Balance Sheet (p.174) shows IDR 742,645,974,247. The Balance Sheet is authoritative; the difference is a PDF OCR artifact in the "Uang muka setoran modal" figure.
7. **Cash trajectory:** Cash fell from IDR 40.1B (2023) to IDR 18.5B (2024) before soaring to IDR 6.16T (2025). The 2025 spike reflects the PMTHMETD I capital raise and bond/sukuk issuances.
8. **Margin expansion:** Gross margin improved dramatically from 39.1% (2023) → 61.7% (2024) → 67.9% (2025), reflecting revenue mix shift toward higher-margin services.
9. **Equity pages 2025:** Pages 240–241 of the 2025 Annual Report use a mirrored/rotated layout (see `lesson.md` Lesson 3). Opening balance was reconstructed; corrected in Run 2 using the 2024 Annual Report.
10. **2023 CF: Related party receivable:** "Penerimaan dari piutang pihak berelasi" = IDR 120.4B collected in 2023 (full repayment of intercompany loan). Included in Net CF Investing but has no dedicated template row.
